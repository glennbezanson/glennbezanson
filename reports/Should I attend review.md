## Tempting, but skip

- **All Evanta summits and Inner Circles (8 entries) and ORBIE.** Eligibility is C-level at $500M+ companies; your only route is paying.
- **FutureCon, Official Cybersecurity Summit, Atlanta Technology Summit.** Sponsor-delivered talks; you would be the audience for pitches.
- **Millennium Alliance assemblies, CDO Magazine, HMG, COLLIDE.** Hosted-buyer or sponsor-required formats: the pay-to-play you asked to avoid.
- **NeurIPS satellite.** Research-grade, not governance.
- **Scheller AI for Business ($2,495).** Literacy you already have.
- **GuidePoint GPSEC.** Run by a competing reseller.
- **Vendor tours**: wrong stack or sales setting.

## Over-rated and under-rated

**Over-rated in the report**
- *FabCon* ("High"). Large and expensive; Fabric is adjacent to your gateway and governance work, not central.
- *Atlanta AI Week.* The 2027 date may be a misread 2026 page, and the security track is sponsor-presented.
- *SecureWorld* ("worth attending"). The valuable part is the invite-only Advisory Council roundtable, which you only reach by speaking.

**Under-rated**
- *The Delta site visit.* A rare look at production AI inside an Atlanta enterprise.
- *The Holiday Party.* One evening replaces a dozen exploratory meetups.
- *ALIGN AI Executive Summit.* Closed to vendors, but NetApp is an Edge partner: ask for a guest seat. Do not pay.
- *The five deadlines.* For your goals, speaking beats attending. The Azure in the ATL (Jan 30), OAI (Feb 28) and DevOpsDays (Jan 19, aggregator-only date) calls matter more than most events here.
