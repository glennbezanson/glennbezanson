Research date: 2026-11-15. Method note for the report writer: official calendar pages were opened and read where they loaded (noted as "page opened"). Items known only from a search-result summary are marked "search snippet only". The web-search budget for this session ran out before every sub-topic was searched; un-searched sub-topics are listed under Gaps in each section and are NOT evidence that no event exists. ## Key Question 1: Georgia Tech (Tech AI, ML@GT, SCP, GTRI, IDEaS, Scheller, IPaT, Ivan Allen, Nunn School, CREATE-X, ATDC, NSF AI institutes, Georgia AIM)

### Takeaway
Georgia Tech is the densest source of small, expert-level events and most of them are never on the "big 5" lists: the SCP Thursday security talks (Coda Atrium), the ML@GT Wednesday seminar, the IPaT/GVU lunch lectures at Tech Square, the Ivan Allen "AI & Society" evening series (two confirmed 2027 dates), the Nunn School AI/national-security forums, and GTRI's ASCCENT AI Security Summit (held Dec 3, 2025; no 2026 edition was findable as of Nov 15, 2026). The single best aggregator is the Tech AI events feed (ai.gatech.edu / techai.gatech.edu), which cross-lists AI events from every GT unit.
