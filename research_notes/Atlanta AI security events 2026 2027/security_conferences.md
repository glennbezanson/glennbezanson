## What are ALL the security conferences and community events held in metro Atlanta?

### Takeaway
I verified about 45 distinct events or groups active in 2025-2026, well beyond the usual five; the richest AI-security content per dollar is at BSides Atlanta (about 20 AI talks in 2026, $50-60), OWASP Atlanta (monthly, AI-heavy, free) and ISSA Metro Atlanta (monthly, free, 6 of the last 14 meetings on AI). The long tail is mostly invite-style executive dinners and vendor summits that appear on Luma, not on conference calendars.
