# AI-Focused Conferences, Summits and Multi-Session Events in Metro Atlanta (Nov 2026 - Dec 2027)

Research date: 2026-11-15. Scope: conferences, summits, annual/one-off multi-session events (not monthly meetups; not pure security conferences).

Verification legend used throughout:
- **[P]** = I fetched the organizer's own page (or the organizer's Sessionize/Eventbrite/GDG page) and read the detail there.
- **[S]** = detail came from a search-result summary of the organizer's page; I did not open the page myself.
- **[A]** = aggregator / third-party listing only; treat as unverified.
- "CONFIRMED" date = stated on an organizer-controlled page. "ESTIMATED" = my projection from past cadence.

Method caveat: the web-search budget for this session was exhausted before I finished. Roughly 45 searches and 40 page fetches were run. Verticals that were queued but NOT searched are listed in the Gaps of the first section. Several organizer sites blocked fetching (ACM AI Summit, Atlanta AI Week Sched, TAG calendar, SSON/AI Data & Analytics Network, atlcloudconf.com).

## What are ALL the AI-focused conferences/summits in Atlanta in 2025-2027?

### Takeaway
There are at least 45 distinct AI / data / enterprise-tech conference-style events in metro Atlanta, far more than the usual five, and the most relevant ones for an enterprise AI governance lead that typical lists miss are the Optimized AI Conference (the renamed Southern Data Science Conference), the TAG AI Summit, Atlanta Cloud+AI Conference, NeurIPS 2026's Atlanta satellite site (Dec 9-13, 2026), Agentic AI Week USA, Avant South's invite-only Applied AI Summit, and the invitation-only executive circuit (Evanta CDAO, CDO Magazine, ALIGN AI, COLLIDE). Confirmed forward dates exist for only about a dozen of them; most 2027 dates are not yet published.
