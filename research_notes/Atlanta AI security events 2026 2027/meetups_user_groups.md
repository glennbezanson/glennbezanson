# Atlanta recurring meetups, user groups and community gatherings (AI / ML / LLM / data / cloud-Azure / AI security) — long-tail inventory, checked 2026-11-15

**Method and limits (read first).** Every group below marked "verified" was opened directly (Meetup, Luma, chapter site) on 2026-11-15; RSVP/attendee counts are what the page displayed that day. Meetup pages show dates without a year; they are read as 2026 because they sit in the page's "upcoming"/"past" lists relative to today. Meetup's public page shows only the 2-4 most recent past events, so "3 most recent events" is sometimes 2. AI Tinkerers "+N" figures are avatar-overflow counters, so treat them as approximate attendance. The shared web-search budget for this research session ran out after the first ~20 searches, so discovery after that point relied on fetching known/guessed URLs; groups I could not reach are listed under Gaps as "not checked", not as dead. `bsidesatl.org` did not resolve from this session (network allowlist or wrong domain).

Verdict labels: **A-S** = active and substantive; **A-L** = active but light; **DORMANT** = no event in roughly 3-6+ months; **DEAD** = no events in years or group page gone; **VIRTUAL-SPAM** = "Atlanta"-branded group whose feed is cross-posted national webinars.

## 1. What are ALL the active AI/ML/LLM (plus data and cloud/Azure) meetups in Atlanta?

### Takeaway
Beyond AI Tinkerers, the real practitioner long tail is: the Alpharetta "Improving office" Microsoft cluster (Azure in the ATL, Atlanta .NET UG, Atlanta MS BI, Atlanta Azure Data UG — all monthly, in person, 16-50 people), Mond(AI)y Coffee at ATDC (weekly), Wednesd.AI at Atlanta Tech Village (weekly), Atlanta Generative AI Group / atl-ai demo nights (monthly, 30-60), The AI Collective Atlanta's neighborhood socials (Sandy Springs, Fayetteville, Douglasville, Tech North), AI Builders at Tech North Atlanta, AI Builders Lunch & Learn at ATL BitLab, PyATL, and the Low-Key Data happy hour. A large share of "Atlanta AI" Meetup groups with thousands of members are webinar cross-post feeds or dead and should be ignored.

### Inferences
- The Improving office in Alpharetta (11675 Rainwater Dr) is the de facto home of Atlanta's Microsoft practitioner community: four separate monthly groups plus the AICamp in-person night and the Snowflake UG meet there. One venue covers most Azure-relevant gatherings, and the attendance band (16-52) matches the requester's "10-30 real practitioners" preference.
- Member counts are a poor signal. The groups with 3,000-5,000 members are disproportionately webinar feeds; the best small groups have under 1,500 members (atl-ai, Low-Key Data, AI Ship & Social) or no member count at all (Mond(AI)y Coffee, Wednesd.AI).

- Three different things are called "AI Builders" in Atlanta (Tech North Atlanta evening meetup, ATL BitLab lunch, and an online-only Meetup group); they should be kept distinct.
